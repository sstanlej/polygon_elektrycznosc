# 2. Overview
![Current concept art](./img/game_concept.png)
## 2.1. High Concept
You are a mage that fights waves of robots. You use elemental magic spells to influence the environment to destroy your enemies.
You collect Junk from killed enemies, with which you unlock more advanced spells. 
Advanced spells, that were unlocked, can be found in chests during gameplay.

## 2.2. Design Pillars
1. **[Pillar 1]:** Elemental spell queue
2. **[Pillar 2]:** Waves of enemies
3. **[Pillar 4]:** Comboing spells
4. **[Pillar 5]:** Player should not be able to kill enemies directly
